Congratulations to Greg on winning the Science faculty prize for the best MSc thesis in Biology. In his study, Greg used two non-invasive sampling methods, scat detection dogs and camera traps, to investigate the influence of small structures on stoat population in agricultural landscapes. His findings show that stoat abundance increases with the presence of small structures, and that stoats prefer stone piles over branch piles. It also highlights the importance of building large structures. The study has been published in the Journal for Nature Conservation.
