In two successive studies, we have been able to show that simple measures to promote biodiversity, such as the installation of branch and stone piles, boost stoat populations in agricultural landscapes. This is particularly true if the measures are professionally supervised, planned and coordinated. The results provide an important impetus for agricultural and nature conservation policies.
