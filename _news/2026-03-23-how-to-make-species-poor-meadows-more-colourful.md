To restore the biodiversity of meadows, less intensive management is not always enough. Sometimes meadows also need to be actively resown. In a research project co-financed by the Swiss National Science Foundation, we tested several methods to restore plant diversity in species-poor meadows on the Swiss Plateau. After four years, all methods proved effective, increasing the number of plant species by an average of 29%.
